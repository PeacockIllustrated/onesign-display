01

Three Jobs,
One Screen

Most venues need their boards to do more than one thing — and that is exactly where the wheels come off.

A food board, a coffee and drinks list, and a promotion are three different jobs. They change on different rhythms, they are designed differently, and they rarely need to be on screen at the same time. Handled manually, each one adds friction.

  • Food prices change, so the whole board goes back to a designer
  • The coffee list is stable but the layout is fiddly to typeset
  • Promotions are urgent and short-lived — and always needed today
  • Breakfast, lunch and evening boards get swapped by hand, or not at all
  • Nobody can see what is actually playing on which screen right now
  • Every site drifts off-brand as staff improvise their own content

The Solution

Onesign Display separates the design from the content. We build your boards once, to your brand. After that your team edits words and prices in a form, sets when each board plays, and the platform handles the rest — on every screen, in every site, automatically.

Designed by Onesign. Edited by your team.
Scheduled once, then it runs itself.

Getting It
On the Wall

There is no proprietary media player to buy. A screen is paired with a token, and anything that can open a web page can be a player — which means you can often use hardware you already own.

Smart TVs

Most modern TVs with a built-in browser can run a screen directly, with no extra box behind it.

Streaming sticks

Fire TV, Android TV boxes and similar low-cost sticks are a reliable, cheap option per screen.

Mini PCs

Where a site already has a PC or NUC driving a display, it simply opens the player URL.

Supplied and installed

Or Onesign supplies the screens, brackets and hardware and installs the lot — our day job.
